# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-09-28

First public release.

- **Library** (`engsep`, 11 modules): vapour-liquid equilibrium with its own Antoine and Wilson data; flash;
  binary, shortcut, multicomponent and batch distillation; absorption and packed towers; liquid-liquid
  extraction on triangular coordinates and leaching; adsorption and fixed-bed breakthrough; membranes (gas,
  reverse osmosis, ultrafiltration, dialysis); crystallization; drying, evaporation and psychrometrics; mass-
  transfer fundamentals; bundled datasets.
- **Course**: 18 executed notebooks with learning objectives, "Inside the algorithm" sections and exercises,
  and 18 notebooks of worked solutions (54 exercises).
- **Validation**: 140 checks against analytic solutions, published data and internal consistency between
  methods.
